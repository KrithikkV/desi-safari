Sivasagr

Step into the royal past of the Ahom Dynasty at Sivasagar, where massive red-brick monuments tell the story of a kingdom that ruled for 600 years. Marvel at the Rang Ghar, one of Asia’s oldest surviving amphitheaters, and the towering Sivadol, one of the tallest Shiva temples in India. The city is dotted with architectural wonders like the multi-storied Kareng Ghar palace and vast man-made tanks like Joysagar, reflecting the engineering brilliance of a dynasty that famously resisted Mughal invasion. Walking through these ruins, you traverse a golden chapter of North East India's history.
