Nalanda

Step back in time at Nalanda, home to the world’s first residential international university that flourished for over 700 years. Walking through these massive red-brick ruins, you can still see the remains of grand monasteries, ancient lecture halls, and the iconic pyramid-shaped Sariputra Stupa. It was once a global intellectual hub where thousands of scholars from across Asia gathered to study philosophy and science. Today, this UNESCO World Heritage site offers a peaceful journey through one of the greatest chapters of India's educational history.
