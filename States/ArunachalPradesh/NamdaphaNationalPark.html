Namdapha National Park

Immerse yourself in the untamed wilderness of Namdapha, India’s third-largest national park and a global biodiversity hotspot. This impenetrable virgin forest is the only place on Earth where you can find four species of big cats—the Tiger, Leopard, Clouded Leopard, and Snow Leopard—all sharing the same habitat. From the lowland evergreen rainforests to the snow-clad peaks of the Dapha Bum, every step reveals rare treasures like the elusive Hoolock Gibbon and the majestic Great Indian Hornbill. A journey through Namdapha is more than a wildlife safari; it is a trek into one of the most pristine and prehistoric ecosystems remaining in the world.
